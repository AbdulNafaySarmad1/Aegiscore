# Nocturne Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Nocturne Systems
Pakistan-native university ERP for regulated academic operations.

## Slide 2: The Problem
Universities run admissions, exams, finance, HR, OBE, and student services across disconnected systems.

## Slide 3: Why Now
HEC reporting, accreditation pressure, digital payments, hybrid delivery, and student expectations are converging.

## Slide 4: Current Alternatives
Cloud ERPs, custom CMS rollouts, spreadsheets, and single-module tools.

## Slide 5: Nocturne Difference
Auditable decisions, cloud-agnostic deployment, offline-first mobility, and Pakistan compliance in one platform.

## Slide 6: Admissions
CRM, multi-step applications, document checks, explainable merit, quota allocation, payments, and offer letters.

## Slide 7: Examination
Assessment schemes, marks moderation, SGPA/CGPA, grace approvals, QR hall tickets, transcripts, and retakes.

## Slide 8: OBE and Accreditation
PO/CLO mapping, attainment calculations, CIS scanning, HEMIS XML, and evidence dashboards.

## Slide 9: Finance and Payroll
GL, procurement, fees, scholarships, PIFRA, EOBI, FBR tax slabs, PF, gratuity, and bank exports.

## Slide 10: Portals and Mobile
Student and faculty portals plus offline-first mobile apps for attendance, marks, notifications, and support.

## Slide 11: Governance
Hierarchy-aware overrides, anonymous encrypted feedback, license controls, immutable audit logs, and capacity checks.

## Slide 12: Deployment
Docker/Kubernetes, on-prem or cloud, PostgreSQL, Redis, local/S3/Azure/SFTP storage, runtime secrets.

## Slide 13: Security
RBAC, SSO, mandatory 2FA, encrypted health records, encrypted anonymous feedback, and no hardcoded secrets.

## Slide 14: Go-To-Market
Pilot with admissions/exams/OBE, expand into finance, HR, portals, mobile, and plugins.

## Slide 15: Ask
Approve a 90-day controlled pilot with registrar, examinations, QEC, finance, and IT stakeholders.
