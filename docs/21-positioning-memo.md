# AegisCore Positioning Memo

## One-Line Positioning

AegisCore is the cloud-agnostic university ERP for Pakistani higher education, built for auditable academic decisions, regulatory reporting, offline mobility, and local finance/payroll compliance.

## Primary Buyer

Vice Chancellors, Registrars, Controllers of Examination, CFOs, Directors IT, and Quality Enhancement Cells.

## Wedge

Start with high-pain regulated workflows: admissions merit, examination/CGPA controls, OBE/CLO reporting, and fee/payroll compliance.

## Proof Points

- Every override is approval-routed and audit-logged.
- Merit decisions are explainable, not opaque.
- OBE evidence and HEMIS exports reduce accreditation scramble.
- On-prem, cloud, and hybrid deployments use the same architecture.
- Faculty attendance and marks workflows keep working offline.

## Message

Generic ERPs digitize forms. AegisCore governs university operations where mistakes become appeals, audit findings, accreditation issues, and fee disputes.
