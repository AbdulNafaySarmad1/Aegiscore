1. Getting Started — Installing the App

iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open the App Store on your iPhone. Its icon is a blue square with a white "A".
  2. Tap the Search tab at the bottom.
  3. Type "AegisCore" in the search box and tap Search.
  4. Look for the app with the shield icon and the name AegisCore ERP by Nocturne Systems (Pvt.) Ltd.
  5. Tap Get or the download icon. You may need to enter your Apple ID password or use Face ID.
  6. The app will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your phone. Its icon is a colourful triangle.
  2. Tap the search bar at the top.
  3. Type "AegisCore" and tap the search icon.
  4. Look for AegisCore University ERP by Nocturne Systems (Pvt.) Ltd.
  5. Tap Install. The app will download and install automatically.
  6. Once installed, tap Open or find the AegisCore icon in your app drawer.
⚠
Watch out There may be similar-looking apps. Make sure the developer is Nocturne Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. before installing.

2. Signing In

  1. Open the AegisCore app. You will see the login screen with the AegisCore shield logo.
  2. Enter your university email address in the first field. This is the email your university gave you (e.g., ahmed.khan@university.edu.pk).
  3. Enter your password in the second field. If you have never logged in before, your university's IT department should have given you a temporary password.
  4. Tap the blue Sign In button.
  5. If this is your first login, you will be asked to create a new password. Choose something you can remember but others cannot guess. Use at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.
  6. Once logged in, you will see your Dashboard.
💡
Pro tip Turn on Biometric Login after signing in. Next time you open the app, just use your fingerprint or face instead of typing your password. See Section 7.

What to do if you forgot your password

  1. On the login screen, tap Forgot Password? below the Sign In button.
  2. Enter your university email address and tap Send Reset Link.
  3. Check your university email inbox (and spam folder) for an email from AegisCore.
  4. Click the link in the email and create a new password.
  5. Return to the app and sign in with your new password.
🚨
Important If you try the wrong password 5 times, your account will be locked for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or contact your university IT support.

4. For Students

Viewing Your Grades

  1. Tap the Academics tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap Grades.
  3. You will see a list of your courses for the current semester. Each row shows: course name, course code, your grade letter, and your marks.
  4. Tap any course to see the full breakdown — midterm marks, final exam marks, assignments, and attendance marks.
  5. To see a previous semester, tap the Semester dropdown at the top and select a different semester.
⚠
Watch out If you believe a grade is wrong, do NOT contact your teacher directly through the app. Use the official Grade Appeal process: go to Academics → Grade Appeal → New Appeal. Fill out the form with your reason and submit. The Grade Appeals Committee will review your case. You will be notified of the decision within 10 working days.

Checking Your Fees

  1. Tap the Finance tab at the bottom.
  2. You will see your Fee Statement showing: Total demanded, Total paid, Outstanding balance.
  3. To pay, tap the blue Pay Now button.
  4. Select your payment method: RAAST (instant bank transfer), Credit/Debit Card, or Bank Challan.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions. For RAAST, you will be redirected to your banking app to approve the payment.
  6. After payment, you will see a green confirmation screen. Tap Download Receipt to save a PDF copy.
🚨
Important If your payment shows as successful but your fee status has not updated after 24 hours, tap Finance → Report Issue and upload your payment receipt. A finance officer will investigate.

Your Attendance

  1. Tap the Academics tab.
  2. Tap Attendance.
  3. You will see a percentage at the top (e.g., "85% Present"). Below that is a list of every class with your attendance status for each day.
  4. Present means you were marked present.
  5. Late means you arrived after attendance was taken.
  6. Absent means you were marked absent that day.
  7. If you need to request leave, tap Request Leave at the top, fill out the form with your dates and reason, and submit.

Mental Health Support (SafeSpace)

💡
This is completely anonymous When you use SafeSpace, no one — not your faculty, not the admin, not anyone — can see your name unless you choose to share it. Your privacy is protected.
  1. Tap the More tab, then SafeSpace.
  2. Tap Submit a Concern.
  3. Choose your concern type from the list (Academic Stress, Anxiety, Family Pressure, Financial Stress, or Other).
  4. Write what you are going through in the text box. Be as detailed as you feel comfortable.
  5. Choose how urgent this is: Routine (I can wait), Urgent (I need help soon), or Crisis (I need help right now).
  6. Tap Submit. You will see a confirmation with a unique code — keep this code if you want to follow up later without revealing your identity.
  7. A counselor will contact you within the timeframe based on your urgency selection (24 hours for Routine, 4 hours for Urgent, immediately for Crisis).
🚨
If you are in crisis RIGHT NOW Do not wait. Tap the Crisis option when submitting your concern — this bypasses the queue and alerts an on-call counselor immediately. You can also call your campus emergency helpline at [Campus Emergency Number] or go to the Student Welfare Office in person during working hours. You are not alone.

5. For Faculty

Taking Attendance

  1. Tap the Academics tab.
  2. Tap My Classes. You will see a list of courses you are teaching this semester.
  3. Tap the course for which you want to take attendance.
  4. Tap Take Attendance.
  5. You will see a list of all enrolled students. Next to each name are three buttons: Present, Late, Absent.
  6. Tap the correct status for each student. By default, all students are marked Present.
  7. When you are done, tap Save Attendance at the bottom. You will see a green confirmation message.
  8. The attendance is now saved and visible to students immediately.

Entering Grades

  1. Tap Academics → Grading.
  2. Select the course from the list.
  3. Choose the assessment component: Midterm, Final Exam, Assignments, or Project.
  4. Enter marks for each student in the fields provided. The maximum marks are shown at the top of the column.
  5. Use the Quick Fill option to mark multiple students with the same score (e.g., if the whole class gets the same attendance mark).
  6. Tap Save Draft if you are not done, or Submit Grades when you are ready to finalize.
⚠
Watch out Once you tap Submit Grades, you cannot change them without filing a grade revision request. Double-check all entries before submitting. The grade submission deadline for this semester is shown in red at the top of the grading screen. Submissions after the deadline require Registrar approval.

Responding to a Grade Appeal

  1. You will receive a notification when a student submits a grade appeal for your course. Tap it to open.
  2. Review the student's stated grounds and any attached evidence.
  3. You have 5 working days to respond. Enter your response in the text box provided.
  4. If you believe the grade was correct, explain why. If you found an error, acknowledge it and suggest the corrected grade.
  5. Tap Submit Response. The Grade Appeals Committee will review both sides and make a final decision.

6. Working Offline (Without Internet)

The AegisCore app works even when you do not have an internet connection. Here is how:

  1. When you are connected to the internet, the app automatically downloads your latest grades, fee status, timetable, and attendance. You do not need to do anything.
  2. If you lose internet connection, a banner will appear at the top saying "Offline Mode — Showing saved data".
  3. You can still view your grades, check your timetable, and see your fee status — all from the last saved version.
  4. If you are a faculty member, you can take attendance while offline. Tap Take Attendance as usual — the marks are saved on your phone.
  5. When you reconnect to the internet, the app will automatically sync all your offline changes. You will see a brief message saying "Syncing..." at the top.
⚠
Watch out Offline attendance must be synced to the server for it to count. Make sure you connect to the internet at least once per day. If you see "Sync pending" badge on your attendance, it means the data has not reached the server yet. Open the app while connected to WiFi to trigger the sync.

7. Biometric Login (Face ID / Fingerprint)

Instead of typing your password every time, you can use your face or fingerprint to log in.

  1. After signing in with your password, tap More → Settings.
  2. Tap Security.
  3. Turn on Biometric Login.
  4. Confirm your password to enable this feature.
  5. Next time you open the app, you will see the biometric prompt: "Use Face ID to sign in" on iPhone or "Use fingerprint to sign in" on Android.
  6. Look at your phone (iPhone) or place your finger on the sensor (Android). The app unlocks instantly.
💡
Pro tip You will still need your password after restarting your phone, after 48 hours of inactivity, or if you add a new fingerprint or face to your device. This is a security feature, not a bug.

8. Push Notifications

The app sends you alerts for important events. The first time you open the app, it will ask: "AegisCore would like to send you notifications." Tap Allow so you do not miss anything.

You will receive notifications for:

  • New grades published
  • Fee payment due or overdue
  • Enrollment confirmation
  • Class schedule changes
  • Grade appeal updates
  • Leave request approved or denied
  • SafeSpace counselor response
  • University-wide announcements

To manage which notifications you receive: More → Settings → Notifications. Toggle on or off for each category.

9. Switching Between Roles

If you have multiple roles at the university (e.g., you are both a Student taking courses and a Teaching Assistant teaching a lab), you can switch between them:

  1. Tap your profile picture or name at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap Switch Role.
  3. Select the role you want to use: Student or Faculty.
  4. The app will reload with the dashboard and menu for that role.
  5. Your data is kept separate for each role. Grade entries made as Faculty will not appear when you switch to Student view.

11. NidaBot — Voice Assistant

NidaBot lets you talk to AegisCore in English or Urdu instead of tapping through menus.

  1. Tap the microphone icon at the bottom-right of any screen.
  2. Speak your question clearly. You can speak in English or Urdu.
  3. NidaBot will understand and respond with the answer on screen and out loud.

Things you can ask NidaBot:

EnglishUrduWhat happens
"What are my grades?""Mera result kya hai?"Shows your grade list
"How much fee do I owe?""Fees kitni baki hai?"Shows outstanding balance
"What is my schedule today?""Ajj mera schedule kya hai?"Shows today's timetable
"What is my attendance?""Meri attendance kaisi hai?"Shows attendance percentage
"I need my roll number slip""Roll number slip chahiye"Generates document

Pro tip If NidaBot does not understand you, say "Dobara bolein" (Repeat) and ask again. Say "Dhire bolein" (Speak slowly) if the response is too fast. Say "Aasan alfaaz mein" (In simple words) for simplified language.

ProblemWhat to do

2. Update the app from the App Store or Play Store.
3. If it still crashes, delete and reinstall the app.
"Cannot connect to server" message1. Check your internet connection — open a website in your browser to test.
2. Switch from mobile data to WiFi, or vice versa.
3. Wait a few minutes and try again — the server may be temporarily down for maintenance.
Grades or fees not showing1. Pull down on the screen to refresh.
2. If still blank, the data may not have been published yet. Check with your department or finance office.

2. Go to Finance → Report Issue and upload your bank's transaction confirmation.
3. Finance staff will verify the payment within 24 hours.

2. In the AegisCore app, go to More → Settings → Notifications and check your preferences.
App shows data in wrong languageGo to More → Settings → Language and select your preferred language.

13. Technical Reference (IT Admins)

App Architecture

The AegisCore mobile app is built as two platform-specific native apps and one cross-platform React Native (Expo) app:

PlatformTechnologyBuild System
React Native (iOS + Android)Expo SDK, TypeScript, Redux, WatermelonDBEAS Build
Android NativeKotlin, Jetpack Compose, GradleGitHub Actions → Play Store
iOS NativeSwift, SwiftUI, Swift Package ManagerXcode / EAS Build

API Connection

The mobile app connects to the AegisCore backend API over HTTPS. Configure the API base URL in:

  • React Native: react-native/src/api/client.ts — set AEGISCORE_API_URL
  • Android: android/app/src/main/kotlin/pk/edu/aegiscore/mobile/Api.kt — set BASE_URL
  • iOS: ios/AegisCore/ApiClient.swift — set baseURL

Building for App Stores

# React Native (Expo) — both platforms
cd react-native
npm install
eas build --platform ios
eas build --platform android

# Android Native
cd android
./gradlew assembleRelease
# APK at: android/app/build/outputs/apk/release/

# iOS Native
cd ios
swift build -c release

Environment Variables

VariableRequiredDescription

PUSH_NOTIFICATION_KEYYesFirebase Cloud Messaging server key

Offline Database

The app stores data locally using:

  • React Native: WatermelonDB (SQLite-based, lazy-loading)
  • Android: Room (SQLite abstraction)
  • iOS: Core Data

Offline changes are queued and synced when connectivity is restored. The sync queue is processed in FIFO order with conflict resolution handled server-side. If a sync conflict occurs, the server version wins and the app downloads the latest data.

Post-Quantum Security

All API communication between the mobile app and the backend uses Hybrid PQC transport: ML-DSA (FIPS 204) post-quantum signatures for authentication alongside TLS 1.3. This means the connection is secure against both classical and quantum computer attacks.
