# AegisCore Capabilities

AegisCore is a university ERP and campus operating platform. It is built to
serve students first, but it also covers the administrative, academic,
financial, compliance, and operational workflows that surround the student
lifecycle.

The portal is the user-facing surface. The .NET backend remains the source of
truth for identity, access control, business rules, workflows, persistence,
and integration logic.

## What AegisCore Covers

### Student Experience

AegisCore gives students a single place to see and act on the parts of campus
life that affect them day to day.

- Login and workspace access
- Student dashboard and role-aware navigation
- Fee status, payment actions, and financial summaries
- Attendance views and academic progress
- Transcript and result access
- Profile and identity information
- Placement and career-readiness views
- Achievement and recognition tracking
- Academic integrity and appeal workflows

### Academic Operations

The platform supports the academic processes that usually sit across several
separate systems.

- Course and class visibility
- Grade appeals and committee review flows
- Grade distribution analysis
- Plagiarism detection and document review
- Proctoring and online exam oversight
- Prerequisite conflict review
- Convocation and academic milestone workflows
- Semester freeze and deferral handling
- Curriculum and catalog ingestion
- Class generation and LMS-related coordination
- Placement readiness, partner company management, and student risk review

### Finance and Fees

AegisCore includes finance workflows that cover both institutional control and
student-facing finance interactions.

- Finance dashboard and executive reporting
- Department finance views
- Fee collection funnels and overdue tracking
- Cash position and budget movement
- Payment gateway configuration
- Voucher and receipt verification
- Student payment actions
- Scholarship and aid visibility
- Cash-flow forecasting and default-risk prediction
- Finance audit and compliance support

### Administration and Governance

The portal is also designed for staff who need oversight across the institution.

- University-wide command center
- Role-scoped workspace navigation
- Governance and board-level views
- Compliance and audit dashboards
- Evidence packages and control reviews
- Access management and setup flows
- System onboarding and installation paths
- Replication, sync lag, and operational health monitoring

### Student Support and Welfare

AegisCore includes operational support surfaces for services that affect
student wellbeing and retention.

- Mental health ticketing and counselor queues
- Anonymous reporting
- Mandatory escalation and crisis workflows
- Transport management
- Cafeteria and vendor scorecard processes
- Foreign student management
- Support for awards, interventions, and readiness monitoring

### Platform and Operations

The portal sits on top of a broader application platform that handles
cross-cutting operational concerns.

- Authentication and session-aware routing
- Multi-role navigation and workspace scoping
- Locale-aware messages
- Shell-based layout and shared portal structure
- API-driven data loading
- Audit-friendly access patterns
- Health, setup, and rollout surfaces

## Core Design Principles

- Students should see the most important information quickly.
- Staff should work from role-scoped screens instead of generic dashboards.
- Sensitive workflows should stay behind authenticated, server-enforced rules.
- Finance, academic, and compliance data should remain auditable.
- The portal should present a coherent workspace, not disconnected pages.

## In Practice

For a student, AegisCore can show fee status, attendance, results, transcript
data, achievement progress, placement readiness, and support access.

For faculty and academic staff, it can manage grade appeals, exam workflows,
proctoring, catalog ingestion, class generation, and integrity reviews.

For finance and administration, it can surface collections, budgets, audit
controls, compliance evidence, role-based access, and operational status.

For leadership, it can consolidate the institution into one command center
with measurable academic, financial, and operational visibility.

## Summary

AegisCore is not just a student portal. It is a full university operating
environment that connects student services, academic operations, finance,
governance, and support workflows in one system.
